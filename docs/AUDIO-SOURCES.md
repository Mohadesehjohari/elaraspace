# Elara Space · Natural ambience sound sources

All natural recording links below point to files hosted by **Wikimedia Commons**. They are loaded only when a visitor presses Play (never autoplayed), remain subject to browser codec support, internet connection and Wikimedia hosting availability, and have a quiet synthesized fallback. They are not claimed to be recordings made by Elara Space. The 3D character voice is not connected.
